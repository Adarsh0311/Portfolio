About

I'm a software engineer with experience building enterprise compliance platforms, university applications, and research tools. My core work spans Java/Spring Boot, AWS, and full-stack development with React and Angular.

At COSMOS Research Center at UA Little Rock, I work on DataVerse, connecting data collection services through a shared interface. Previously, I built integrations and compliance workflows at qordata and Canvas LMS applications at Northwest Missouri State University.

I focus on useful APIs, secure access to data, and automated delivery. My current research work explores how to discover better search keywords for social-media data collection.
